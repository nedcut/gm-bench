Do lost assets really cost future wins? · both teams handed to the same manager for 5 more seasons, same dice (continuation_pick-trader.json)

How it works: rebuild each final league from its ledger, hand both the model's team and pick-trader's team on the same seed to one scripted manager, and keep simulating seasons 6–10. The simulator draws its dice on a fixed schedule, so both continuations get identical luck. A slope of 1 means the asset terms are priced right. The fit runs about 35 points below the dashed line with either manager (strategic gives slope 0.95, offset −34): pick-trader leaves future value no term captures. Picks and hidden potential were ruled out. Model order doesn't depend on it, but "beats pick-trader" does: valued five seasons ahead, astra's +44 becomes about +5.

Plug in, deep dive, or overhaul?

decision
The key fact: replays are exact. Every scoring question can be answered from the ledgers we already have, for free. Paying to rerun models only teaches us something when the simulator changes. So: perfect the scoring offline, bundle the simulator fixes, then pay for one rerun round.

Plug in v2, no reruns

  • Shadow column on the 2.0 rows
  • Fixes playoff noise and the recent-wins triple count
  • Leaves the records bug
Free and safe, but stops halfway.
Chosen

Targeted deep dive → 2.1

  • Free checks, each with a stopping rule
  • Fix the simulator, recalibrate on scripted agents
  • Pre-register, then rerun 3–4 models
The only path whose paid run tests something new.

Full overhaul

  • Redesign terms and weights
  • Best-of series plus a new score
  • Rerun the whole panel
Nothing shows the structure is broken; tuning against the same 11 rows invites overfitting.

How much headroom is above pick-trader?

docs/score_ceiling.mdresults/diagnostics/score_ceiling/
For one seed the simulator is a deterministic function of the GM's 20 decisions, so a per-seed optimum exists. We bracketed it with two searches: a clairvoyant beam search that sees the dice, and a fair planner that resamples everything a GM can't observe.

Lift over pick-trader · public seeds 101–124 (fair replicated on 125–172)

"Luck-neutral" swaps realized playoff results for their exact expectation. Seeing the dice is worth ~130 points, almost all of it playoffs. With playoff luck removed, clairvoyance is worth ~41 and a fair planner gets ~26 of it, ahead on 22 of 24 seeds and on 44 of 48 fresh seeds. About 64% of score variance turned out to be playoff dice, which led to score-v2.
